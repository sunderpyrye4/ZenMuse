About Us

Founded with a vision to transform American homes into sanctuaries of style and comfort, Zen Muse brings over a decade of expertise in home décor consultancy. Our team of certified interior designers specializes in creating spaces that balance aesthetic appeal with functional living, serving clients across all 50 states.

We believe that your home should be a reflection of your personality while providing a tranquil retreat from the outside world. Our signature approach combines the timeless elegance of graphite black and ice white with contemporary steel accents, creating interiors that are both sophisticated and welcoming.

Whether you're refreshing a single room or undertaking a complete home transformation, Zen Muse provides the guidance, resources, and creative vision to bring your dream space to life. Our philosophy centers on creating harmonious environments that promote wellbeing through thoughtful design, quality materials, and personalized attention to detail.

Our Services

Full Home Design

Comprehensive interior design services for your entire home, creating cohesive spaces that flow seamlessly from room to room with our signature color palette.

Room Transformation

Focused redesign of specific rooms like living rooms, bedrooms, or kitchens to maximize both function and style using graphite black and steel accents.

Color Consultation

Lighting Design
